About Us

The Boeing National Aeromodelling Festival brings student builders and flyers from across the country to one field for a few days of competition and learning.

It is organised by the Aeromodelling Club, IIT Kanpur. Whether you have flown for years or never launched a glider, there is a place for you here.

Build

Design and construct your own aircraft, from foam gliders to powered fixed-wing models.

Fly

Compete in flight events judged on performance, precision and craftsmanship.

Learn

Join sessions led by experienced modellers and engineers, then test what you learned in the air.

Workshop

Pick a session, learn by building, and take your skills straight to the flight line.

Fixed-wing basics

Learn how wings, tails and weight distribution decide how a model flies. You will build and trim a small aircraft, then fly it.

Radio and electronics setup

Set up transmitters, receivers, servos, motors and batteries correctly and safely, and learn to fix common faults.

Multirotor and FPV

Understand how multirotors are controlled and tuned, with an introduction to first-person-view flying.

Aerodynamics for builders

Lift, drag and stability explained with simple experiments, so you can improve your next design.
